Concept & Mission

What is a Scan Party?

A fun, educational, and impactful workshop combining social teamwork with citizen science: decipher food labels while enriching the world's largest open food commons.

📊

Enrich Open Food Facts

Add new products and refresh ingredient lists, nutrition tables, and recyclable packaging specs.

🛡️

Empower Consumers

Provide verified, transparent facts to help everyone make healthier and more sustainable food choices.

🤝

Promote Collaboration

Connect neighbors, students, coworkers, and co-op members through engaging, collaborative teamwork.

🌱

Raise Food Literacy

Demystify Nutri-Score, Eco-Score, NOVA ultra-processing groups, and additive classifications.

2 Flexible Workflows

2 Ways to Run Your Scan Party

Whether you have high-speed Wi-Fi in a classroom or zero cellular reception in a supermarket basement, Open Food Facts has you covered!

Best with Wi-Fi / 4G

Method 1: Live Mobile App Scanning

Participants install the free Open Food Facts mobile app, scan items, and add photos directly on their phones.

  • 1
    Install Free App Available on iOS and Android without ads or paywalls.
  • 2
    Pair Up Participants One person handles products, the second scans barcodes and captures labels.
  • 3
    Capture 4 Key Angles Front packaging, ingredients list, nutrition table, and recycling specs.
  • 4
    Instant AI & Score Calculation Built-in OCR extracts text and computes Nutri-Score & Eco-Score on the spot!
📱 Download Open Food Facts App
No Wi-Fi Needed!

Method 2: Offline Photos & Producer Import

When scanning in basements with no Wi-Fi, take photos using standard camera phones following the barcode anchor sequence, then bulk upload the folder to the Producer Platform!

  • 1
    Sequential Camera Photos Use standard camera phones to snap clear photos.
  • 2
    Barcode Photo First The barcode photo serves as the separator delimiter for automatic pairing.
  • 3
    Collect Photo Folders Gather photo folders onto the host laptop after the event.
  • 4
    Drag & Drop Bulk Upload Upload the complete folder to pro.openfoodfacts.org for batch processing!
🌐 Visit Producer Platform (pro.openfoodfacts.org)

The 'Barcode Anchor' Photo Sequence Protocol

Ordre strict obligatoire

For offline scanning, follow this strict sequence for each product: the barcode photo acts as the delimiter so the system correctly groups consecutive packaging photos.

Step 1 • Anchor
📶
1. Barcode Photo

Flat and sharp with visible digits (acts as delimiter).

📦
2. Front Packaging

Product name, brand, logos, and net weight/volume.

📋
3. Ingredients List

Full ingredients list and allergen statements.

🥗
4. Nutrition Facts

Values per 100g / 100ml (calories, sugars, fats, salt).

♻️
5. Packaging & Eco

Recycling symbols (Triman), material types (PET, HDPE, PP).
