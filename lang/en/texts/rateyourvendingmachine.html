Citizen Science Campaign

Grade Your Vending Machine!

Ever wondered what is really inside the vending machine on your train platform, university campus, or office break room? Discover its nutritional quality at a glance.

Bringing transparency to everyday snack choices

Launched as an initiative by Open Food Facts, this campaign empowers citizens to evaluate and reveal the nutritional quality of vending machines. Thanks to the Nutri-Score system (5 color letter grades from A to E based on the research of Prof. Serge Hercberg), anyone can ascertain the nutritional quality of food products in a single glance.

The « Grade Your Vending Machine! » (#TagYourVendingMachine) action raises awareness on the lack of product diversity in automated machines. Too often, vending machines are filled exclusively with highly processed, sugary, and salty snacks with few or no healthy alternatives. By grading whole machines, we bring collective transparency to these shared spaces without anyone needing to spend money to find out!

The nutritional computation takes into account saturated fats, sugars, sodium, energy, protein, fibers, and fruit/vegetable content — computed collaboratively on millions of products worldwide in our open database.

A vending machine labeled with Nutri-Score color ratings for every snack slot.

How can you participate?

Join thousands of citizen contributors in three simple steps.

1

Snap the Machine

Come across a vending machine at work, school, or a transit station? Take a clear, well-lit frontal photo showing the rows of products and selections.

2

Tag & Share

Share the photo on social networks with the hashtag #TagYourVendingMachine (or #NoteTonDistrib), tagging @OpenFoodFacts, or scan items directly via the app.

3

Spread Awareness

Get back the machine photo mapped with Nutri-Score ratings. Share the graded breakdown in your company, school, or even post it by the machine to encourage healthier options!
