"Opening Up Food Information: An Independent Citizen Counter-Power to Inform Public Debate"

Open Food Facts is immensely proud and grateful to be backed by the Fondation de France with a catalytic 150,000 € grant. This ambitious project aims to permanently anchor an independent digital commons, empowering citizens, researchers, consumer defense groups, and journalists to illuminate individual dietary decisions and elevate societal debate.

"As we have observed for over a decade, a data contributor becomes an informed data user: citizens develop direct familiarity with food composition, marketing claims, and environmental realities, becoming active participants in digital democracy."

Equipping citizens with open Scan Party organizer kits and running participatory thematic campaigns. Building a direct bridge between academic researchers, investigative journalists, and volunteer contributors for time-constrained field investigations.

Horizon Europe Project • 2025–2029

Opération Foodture: Building Europe's Environmental Food Commons

Expanding scientific environmental life-cycle assessment (LCA) across 31 European countries through grassroots product coverage.

Pan-European Impact

Scaling Environmental Scoring from France to All of Europe

While France created the landmark Agribalyse database (developed by ADEME and INRAE) to calculate the Eco-Score / Green-Score, Europe urgently needs a unified, open environmental database. Open Food Facts is a proud partner of the European FOODTURE consortium to fill this critical gap across 31 countries (EU-27 + Norway, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Serbia, and Montenegro).

To compute trustworthy Life-Cycle Assessments (carbon footprint, water usage, agricultural land use, and packaging recyclability), scientific models need exact data on representative food products in hundreds of categories across every single European country. That is where citizen scientists make history!

Civic Economic Transparency

Open Prices & Monthly Community Challenges

From inflation tracking to food justice: turning grocery receipts and shelf prices into open economic commons.

Food transparency is not just about what is inside the box—it is also about whether good food is accessible and fairly priced. With Open Prices (prices.openfoodfacts.org), Open Food Facts extended citizen science to supermarket receipts and shelf price tags. Every month, community members take on targeted sprints to uncover pricing trends, regional inequalities, and shrinkflation.

🎃 October Focus • Challenge #17

Scantober Challenge for Foodture

Our flagship autumn citizen science sprint! Volunteers across Europe scan barcode labels and upload receipts targeting representative staple food categories to feed the pan-European Foodture environmental database.
