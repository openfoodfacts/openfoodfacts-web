Gamification & Editing Tools

Open Food Facts receives thousands of product photographs every day. Our gamification tools turn the tedious work of inspecting and extracting label data into quick, addictive micro-challenges that anyone can play anywhere.

Production Flagship

🏹 Hunger Games

Hunger Games is our real-time, browser-based annotation game. Built with modern React 19, TypeScript, Vite, and Material UI, it allows contributors to validate machine learning insights in seconds—from confirming food categories to spotting obscure eco-labels.

The Mini-Game Modes

Hunger Games is organized into modular mini-games designed for specific data extraction challenges:

🏷️

Logo & Brand Detective

Help detect and identify food brand logos, quality certifications (AB, Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance), and eco-labels cropped by our computer vision neural networks.

Vision & Logos
🗂️

Category Chooser

Confirm or re-classify AI-predicted food categories based on product titles and ingredient lists. One keystroke confirms or rejects a suggestion.

NLP & Taxonomies
📊

Nutri-Sight Table Validator

Verify OCR-extracted nutrition tables (sugars, salt, saturated fat, protein) side-by-side with original photos to ensure 100% Nutri-Score precision.

OCR Verification
♻️

Packaging & Recycling

Identify packaging components (caps, trays, pouches), materials (glass, PP, PET, aluminium), and recycling instructions for Green-Score computations.

Eco-Score / Green-Score
⚖️

Weights & Quantities

Spot package net weight, volume, or piece counts to unlock automatic price-per-kilogram analysis and standardized nutrition computations.

Data Extraction

🤖 The Human-in-the-Loop AI Feedback Loop

Hunger Games is not just a game: it is the human heart of our active learning artificial intelligence engine.

1
Robot Prediction

Robotoff runs OCR, object detection, and NLP models over newly uploaded product packaging photos.

2
Human Verification

Hunger Games serves uncertain predictions to human players as rapid, bite-sized questions.

3
Database Update

Validated facts are instantly committed to the live product database and visible worldwide.

4
Model Retraining

Annotations become labeled training data, continuously training smarter, more accurate models.
