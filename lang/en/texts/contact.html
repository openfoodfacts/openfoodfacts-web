⚠️ Important Notice Regarding Products & Manufacturers

Open Food Facts is an independent, non-profit crowdsourced database. We do not manufacture, sell, distribute, or market any food or consumer products, and we have no commercial relationship with product manufacturers. If you have a complaint, quality issue, or safety concern regarding a specific consumer item, please contact the product's manufacturer directly (using the consumer helpline or postal address on the label) or file a report with your national consumer protection authority.
