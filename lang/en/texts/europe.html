Executive Summary

Open Food Facts in a nutshell

A digital public common equipping scientific research, civil society, industry accountability, and European policy.

An open ecosystem for European food transparency

Open Food Facts is a unique open ecosystem for collecting and sharing data, information, and algorithms related to food and consumer products. Built around an open database of over 4.5 million products (with roughly three-quarters sold in Europe), the Open Food Facts community brings together 80,000+ contributors worldwide, a dedicated core team of 10 employees, and an active ecosystem of thousands of technology and food professionals.

Our community and digital commons equip research and innovation: several hundred peer-reviewed research projects rely on our data, and over 200 mobile and web applications use our open APIs and algorithms—including nutritional and environmental scoring (Nutri-Score, Green-Score, NOVA, Eco-Score)—for public health, educational, and commercial uses.

Traceability & Origins

Defragmenting EU Packager Codes (EC Health Marks)

Across the European Single Market, food packaging must bear an oval health/identification mark (veterinary approval mark) indicating the establishment where the product was manufactured or packed:

FR 75.101.001 CE DE-BY 12345 EG ES 10.00000/B CE IT 03 123 CE NL 1234 EG PL 12345678 WE

While retailers frequently mask the actual producer behind private store brands, national health mark registries are siloed and inaccessible across borders. Open Food Facts crowdsources packaging photos, extracts oval codes with OCR, and cross-references member-state registries to geolocate factories, trace supply chains, and empower citizens with true manufacturing transparency.

Multilingual Engine

Cross-Border Packaging & Multilingual Taxonomies

European products routinely cross borders with multi-language packaging (e.g. French/Dutch in Belgium; German/French/Italian in Switzerland/Luxembourg; German/Austrian distributions).

Open Food Facts dynamically parses multilingual ingredient blocks, automatically normalizes synonyms, and maps ingredients against our open multilingual taxonomy of 100,000+ entries. Additives are indexed by their E-numbers across all official EU languages, allowing accurate allergen warnings and additive risk profiles regardless of the consumer's native language.

The "Facts" Family

Open Data Commons Supporting EU Green & Health Agendas

The Open Food Facts ecosystem extends beyond food to address complementary EU consumer safety and circular economy goals through dedicated open sister databases:

🥗 Open Food Facts (OPF)

Nutri-Score, NOVA ultra-processing, Green-Score lifecycle assessment, food reformulation tracking, anti-waste, and nutritional literacy.

💄 Open Beauty Facts (OBF)

Cosmetics Regulation (EC 1223/2009), INCI chemical ingredients deciphering, allergen alerts, microplastics, and endocrine disruptors.

🐾 Open Pet Food Facts (OPFF)

Pet nutrition, animal feed transparency, companion animal welfare, and veterinary ingredient verification.

📦 Open Products Facts

Non-food consumer goods, repairability index, packaging circularity, and eco-design verification for consumer durables.

💶 Open Prices

Crowdsourced European supermarket price tracking, food inflation monitoring, and purchasing power transparency across Member States.

Policy & Institutional Guide

Information for European Commission staff & EU agencies

Key facts for policy officers at DG SANTE, DG AGRI, DG ENVI, DG CNECT, JRC, and EFSA.

🏛️ How can European institutions and agencies use Open Food Facts data?

All Open Food Facts database contents are published under the Open Database License (ODbL). EU Commission Directorates-General, the Joint Research Centre (JRC), the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), and national agencies can freely download daily dumps, execute bulk queries, or integrate our public APIs for market monitoring, reformulation tracking, environmental assessments, and consumer policy evaluation with zero licensing fees.

🛡️ How does Open Food Facts maintain independence and data integrity?

Open Food Facts is organized as an independent, non-profit non-governmental association (association loi 1901) with strict governance rules preventing corporate lobbying influence. Data contributions are auditable through permanent photographic proofs of physical packaging, with algorithmic anomaly detection (Robotoff) and volunteer moderation preserving scientific reproducibility.

📜 How does Open Food Facts support EU regulatory frameworks?

Our infrastructure directly operationalizes key European regulatory goals: Front-of-Pack Nutrition Labeling (Nutri-Score), the Green Claims Directive (lifecycle environmental verification), Food Waste Reduction targets under the Waste Framework Directive, the EU Cosmetics Regulation (INCI decoding on Open Beauty Facts), and the European Single Market's traceability requirements (EC health approval marks).

🤝 Can Open Food Facts provide customized technical briefings or data extracts for EU institutions?

Yes. Our team regularly presents technical briefings, provides tailored aggregate data extracts for scientific studies, and participates in European workshops on food transparency and citizen science. Contact our institutional liaison desk at contact@openfoodfacts.org.
