⚡ Quick Micro-Contributions

Even if you only have 30 seconds, you can make an immediate impact by answering AI validation questions or selecting and re-cropping photos from the community gallery.

Answer questions to help complete products (Robotoff AI)

Step 1

When logged into your Open Food Facts account, you may see a blue question card on the product sheet. Tap it to answer.

Step 2

The question will appear with three choices: press "Not sure" if in doubt, "No" if incorrect, and "Yes" if you are certain.

Step 3

By validating the suggested food category, Open Food Facts can instantly calculate and display the product's Nutri-Score!

Recropping a previously uploaded photo from the gallery

Gallery Search

If no ingredients photo is selected, check if another photo (like the front packaging) already contains it. Tap the magnifying glass icon to view all uploaded photos.

Photo Selection

In the photo gallery, browse all uploaded pictures for this product and tap the one that best captures the missing information.

Validate Selection

The photo is now designated as the ingredients photo for that language. Tap ✓ to confirm your selection.

Adjusting the crop and orientation of existing pictures

Identify Issue

If a photo is poorly cropped or includes extraneous text, tap on the picture to enter the cropping tool.

Enter Crop Tool

Tap the crop button to adjust the selection box around the nutrition facts or ingredients list.

Adjust Handles

Use the four white corner handles to resize the bounding box. If near the edge of your screen, use the rotate button to orient the area toward the center, then rotate back.

Completed Crop

Congratulations! The nutrition image is now sharply framed, allowing text extraction to run smoothly.

Confirmation

Confirm your cropped image to replace the blurry or poorly framed previous version.

Tight Crop

Crop closely around the ingredients block to ensure the OCR engine won't extract unrelated marketing slogans.

Result

The cropped section of the packaging now provides a dedicated, high-quality ingredients photo.

Background Sync

Cropping instructions and images are uploaded to the Open Food Facts servers seamlessly in the background.

Complete Product Update Walkthrough

Follow the numbered steps below to perform a complete product refresh when you have a physical product in hand with updated packaging or new ingredients.

1 Check for Photo or Data Mismatch

Scan any product with the barcode scanner. Check if the current front photo matches your packaging. Slide up the product card to inspect nutrition facts, ingredients, and categories.

Step 1.1

Scan the barcode. If you spot differences between the app and the physical product, take new photos to empower the entire community!

Step 1.2

Slide up the product summary card to open the complete product details page.

Step 1.3

Tap directly on the product photo to enter the photo management and update screen.

2 Take a New Front Photo

Capture a crisp, well-lit photo of the main packaging face. Avoid shadows and personal items in the frame.

Step 2.1

Tap the camera icon under the Front Photo placeholder to launch the camera viewfinder.

Step 2.2

Frame the product neatly against a neutral or white background whenever possible.

Step 2.3

Review the sharpness and readability. Once satisfied, tap the checkmark to confirm.

Step 2.4

Adjust the crop handles. Prefer a slightly generous crop to ensure no badges, weights, or brands are cut off.

Step 2.5

A spinning indicator confirms upload. You do not need to wait—you can immediately move on to the ingredients!

Step 2.6

The new front photo is now live and associated with the product.

3 Take and Crop Ingredients Photo

Ingredients photos require a precise, high-contrast crop because they are used directly by the automatic optical character recognition (OCR) engine.

Step 3.1

Tap the camera icon for Ingredients. Good lighting is essential for accurate character recognition.

Step 3.2

Take a clear photo of the ingredients list. If the packaging has multiple languages, capture them all—you can crop each language individually.

Step 3.3

Use the four white handles to frame the ingredients text closely. You can rotate the image with ↪️ and ↩️ if needed.

Pro Tip

If edge handles are hard to drag on your screen, rotate the image 90°, adjust the top handles easily, and then rotate back!

Step 3.4

Once the crop covers just the ingredients list, tap ✓ to confirm.

Multilingual

For products with multilingual packaging, select additional languages using the "Languages" dropdown menu.

Language Check

If a selected language has no photo yet, the dropdown appears highlighted in yellow with a reminder note.

Second Language

Re-use the same packaging photo to crop specifically on the second language list (e.g. Dutch, German, Spanish).

Complete

Great job! You have added localized ingredient lists for speakers of multiple languages.

4 Take a New Nutrition Photo

Nutrition tables are often multilingual. A single clear photo encompassing the entire nutrition table is usually sufficient for all languages.

Step 4.1

Select the Nutrition tab and tap the camera icon to start capturing the table.

Step 4.2

Align your camera directly perpendicular to the nutrition table to avoid distortion.

Step 4.3

Ensure energy values (kJ/kcal), fats, carbs, sugars, fiber, proteins, and salt are all legible.

Step 4.4

Crop neatly to include both the nutrient names and the values per 100g and per portion.

Step 4.5

The cropped nutrition table is saved and ready for automated analysis.

Photos Ready

All photos are now refreshed! Tap the back arrow in the upper corner to return to the product screen.

5 Enter Edit Mode & Extract Ingredients via OCR

With new photos uploaded, make sure the text and numerical values reflect the physical packaging. Use automatic OCR to extract the ingredients in seconds!

Step 5.1

Inspect the Nutrition tab to see if recipe values have changed. Tap any photo to zoom in.

Step 5.2

Navigate to the Ingredients tab to compare the listed ingredients with your packaging.

Step 5.3

Tap "Edit" on the Summary tab to enter editing mode for data fields.

Step 5.4

In the Ingredients section, long press on the existing ingredients text to show selection cursors.

Step 5.5

From the context popup menu, tap the three dots (⋮) and tap "Select all".

Step 5.6

Tap "Cut" or backspace to remove the outdated ingredient text.

Step 5.7

Once the field is empty, the "Extract ingredients" button appears. Tap it to trigger OCR!

Step 5.8

The extracted text appears automatically. It often contains prefixes or suffixes from surrounding packaging text.

Step 5.9

Select and delete any prefix text (such as "Ingredients:" or marketing slogans) at the start of the list.

Step 5.10

Select and remove any trailing text (e.g. barcode numbers, manufacturer addresses, or storage instructions).

Crucial OCR Verification Checklist

  • Asterisks (*): Check special markers (such as organic asterisks). Verify that OCR has not mistranscribed them as apostrophes, commas, or letters.
  • Punctuation & Commas: Verify that commas properly separate distinct ingredients (e.g. "salt, pepper" not "salt pepper").
  • Percentages: Verify numbers and percentage signs (e.g. "cocoa butter 32%").
  • Typos: Correct any minor spelling errors or unrecognized characters before validating.
Step 5.11

When everything is verified, tap "This looks Good". (Note: "Too bad, ignore" will discard the extraction).

Step 5.12

A green checkmark appears confirming validation. Be careful: your edits are not yet permanently saved!

Step 5.13

Tap the back arrow to save ingredients immediately, or tap "Next" at the bottom to continue to Nutrition Facts.

6 Update Nutrition Facts

Adjust numerical nutrient values as indicated on the packaging.

Step 6.1

Double check values per 100g / 100mL versus per portion. You can specify the exact portion size (e.g. "30g" or "2 biscuits").

Step 6.2

At the top, you can also toggle the switch if the product legally does not carry nutritional information (e.g. coffee, tea, table salt).

7 Save Your Edits

Finish your update and submit your valuable contributions to the global database.

Step 7.1

Scroll to the bottom of the Nutrition tab and tap the green "Save edits" button.

Step 7.2

A confirmation banner confirms that your updates were successfully synchronized with Open Food Facts!

Step 7.3

The updated product sheet is now live, and Nutri-Score, Eco-Score, and NOVA scores are immediately re-evaluated.

🎉 Congratulations!

You've completed your product update. Every photo, ingredient correction, and nutrition fact you provide helps millions of citizens make healthier and more sustainable food choices every day.
