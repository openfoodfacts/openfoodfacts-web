Territorial Stakes

Why Food Transparency Is a Central Lever for Territories

Diet-related diseases and environmental footprints weigh heavily on local communities (in France alone, 1 in 2 adults is overweight and diet-related costs reach €50B/year for public health insurance). Credible, systematic, and shared product information helps answer three core territorial questions:

🥗

1. Eat Better Everyday

Empower residents, families, and schools with transparent, science-backed indicators (Nutri-Score, NOVA, Eco-Score / Green-Score) so everyone can make healthier, lower-impact food choices.

🌾

2. Produce Better at Low Cost

Give local farmers, artisans, and SMEs free access to our Producer Platform to reference local products, calculate scores, and simulate recipe improvements without costly consulting.

📊

3. Steer Local Food Policies

Leverage fine-grained territorial food and price data for your Territorial Food Projects (PAT), climate plans, public procurement, and local open-data portals.

City of Paris (Mairie de Paris) & Académie du Climat

The City of Paris (Mairie de Paris) has actively collaborated with Open Food Facts across multiple flagship initiatives, including presenting its territorial feedback at the annual Open Food Facts Days:

  • Open Food Facts Days Speaker: Mairie de Paris shared how municipal teams and local facilities mobilize open food data and citizen science for ecological and food transition.
  • Académie du Climat (Paris 4e) — Packaging & Eco-Score Scan Party (November 2023): 22 participants scanned and documented 240 products (packaging materials, recycling symbols, and weights) to improve environmental scoring.

  • Ongoing Parisian Community Events: Including Paris Scan Party Night (2014), the 1st Open Food Facts General Assembly (2015), Franprix Paris Scan Party (2016), and Les 400 Coop (Paris Region, 2023 — 320 products scanned).

Action & Support

How Your Local Government Can Support & Collaborate with Open Food Facts

Whether you are a small municipality, a metropolis, or a regional council, there are concrete, accessible ways to support the food data commons and deploy high-impact actions locally.

📸

Host a Territorial Scan Party

Mobilize residents, schools, youth councils, climate centers, or associations to photograph and reference local food products, packaging, and recycling labels. We provide the methodology, mobile app, and campaign tracking.

  • Showcase local producers and short supply chains
  • Raise citizen awareness on nutrition, ultra-processing (NOVA), and packaging
  • Feed your local Open Data portal with verified product records
🏷️

Launch an Open Prices Collection Action

Support Open Prices by organizing price-collection drives in local supermarkets and markets (following the Paris 3-store pilot model).

  • Monitor food inflation and price disparities across neighborhoods
  • Link price data with nutritional & environmental quality
  • Identify coverage gaps using our territorial gap-map tools
🍽️

Public Catering (Restauration collective) Tools

In public and private collective catering, recipes and ingredients change constantly, making Nutri-Score and Green-Score calculation complex. Collaborate with us on our free, open-source recipe calculator (API or white-label tool) to manage menus, recipes, and labeling.

🤝

Grants, Open Data & Institutional Partnerships

Support Open Food Facts as a digital public infrastructure through project grants, municipal subsidies, co-hosting institutional events like Open Food Facts Days, or commissioning a territorial Dat’Alim program.

Turnkey Territorial Program

Dat’Alim: Mobilizing Citizens, Local Producers & Open Data

Our flagship territorial methodology connects citizens, local authorities, and food producers to map and improve local food supply chains in 4 structured steps:

STEP 01

Pilot Stakeholder Mobilization

Multi-actor workshops to map existing data and select priority territorial use cases (local sourcing, schools, open data).

STEP 02

Collection Preparation

Build a local volunteer group, adapt collection tools, and prepare training kits for local producers.

STEP 03

Territorial Collection & Training

Producer outreach, training sessions on the free Producer Platform, and citizen Scan Parties across the territory.

STEP 04

Analysis & Territorial Showcase

Dedicated local-origin filters & PDO highlights in Open Food Facts apps, "Made near you" maps, Datacamps, and Open Data publication.
