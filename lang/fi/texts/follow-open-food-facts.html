Stay Connected with Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts is a non-profit, collaborative database made by everyone, for everyone. Follow our channels to keep up with product news, healthy eating tips, food science, open data releases, and community campaigns!

The Best Way: Create an Open Food Facts Account

Creating a free account is the surest way to receive our newsletter. Even better, it unlocks the full power of Open Food Facts: you can scan and add products, edit ingredients, calculate Nutri-Score & Eco-Score, and contribute directly to food transparency for all!
