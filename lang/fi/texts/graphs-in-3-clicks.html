How to Create a Graph in 3 Clicks

The Open Food Facts advanced search engine lets you filter and correlate any segment of the global food database.

1
🎯

1. Scope & Filter Products

Combine up to 5 simultaneous criteria: category (e.g. cereals, cheeses), country of sale, brand, labels (Organic, Fair Trade), or Nutri-Score grades.

  • ✔ Categories, brands, ingredients
  • ✔ Country of sale & quality labels
  • ✔ Multi-criteria Boolean filtering
2
📐

2. Select Axes & Visualization

Choose your X and Y axes (sugars vs additives, calories vs fats, sodium vs Nutri-Score). Enable visual markers for organic products or sweeteners.

  • ✔ 2-Axis Scatter plots
  • ✔ Distribution bar charts
  • ✔ Geospatial maps & CSV exports
3
🚀

3. Inspect, Verify & Share

Hover over data points to preview product sheets. Spot nutritional outliers, audit input errors, and share your persistent graph URL with the world.

  • ✔ Dynamic interactive data points
  • ✔ Visual outlier identification
  • ✔ Permanent URL for papers & sharing
💡 Iconic Case Study

Sugars & Additives in Sodas (The Sodas Operation)

This iconic scatter plot correlates sugar content (X axis) with food additives count (Y axis) across sodas collected by the community. Green dots highlight certified organic sodas, while specific markers identify formulations containing intense artificial sweeteners.

Explore this Live Graph ↗

📈 2-Axis Scatter Plots

Correlate any two nutrients or metrics with interactive hover tooltips.

🗺️ Geospatial & Origins Maps

Map manufacturing sites and ingredient provenance (Protected Origins, Fair Trade).

📥 Custom Lists & Data Exports

Download filtered datasets as CSV or Excel for in-depth data analysis (R, Python, LibreOffice).

💡

Community Data Validation Tip

Before publishing or referencing a graph in an article or study, double-check products at extreme outlier points. Input errors can occur: if a value seems unusually high or low, check the original packaging photo on Open Food Facts and edit it directly!
