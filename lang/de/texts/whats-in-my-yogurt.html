The 'Trojan Horse' into Everyday Refrigerators

Why focus on yogurts after sodas? Because yogurt is a household staple enjoyed daily by hundreds of millions of children and families. Yet behind its innocent 'health food' halo, nutrition labels conceal staggering contrasts: synthetic thickeners masking fat-free textures, high-fructose corn syrups, deceptive fruit imagery, and extreme geographical variations.

By inviting everyday shoppers to snap pictures of the yogurt cups in their kitchen or local co-op, Open Food Facts used yogurt as a friendly Trojan Horse to bring open data literacy into household routines.

Key Discoveries of the Global Yogurt Inquiry

🥛 Are yogurt portion sizes identical across different countries?
💡 Surprising cultural gap: standard individual yogurt pots are 125g in France, 180g in Switzerland, and up to 200-250g in the US!
🥛 What percentage of real fruit is actually present in fruit yogurts?
💡 Many commercial yogurts contain under 5% actual fruit preparation, compensated with thickeners, sugar, and natural color extracts.
🥛 Do low-fat (0%) yogurts compensate texture with extra sugar or additives?
💡 Data reveals that fat-free variants frequently replace creaminess with modified starches, pectin, and synthetic intense sweeteners.
🥛 Are yogurts sweeter in certain regions or overseas territories?
💡 Noticeable disparities in added sugars were mapped across local markets, fueling community campaigns for honest formulations.
