Story Angles & Narratives

The Many Stories of Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts is far more than a barcode scanner. It is a digital public commons at the crossroads of public health, planetary boundaries, consumer purchasing power, the circular economy, and civic AI.

🥗 Public Health

The Nutri-Score Revolution & Recipe Reformulation

How an open citizen database validated and pioneered front-of-pack nutritional labeling in Europe, compelling food manufacturers to cut sugar, salt, and saturated fats.

Story Angles:

  • The 2024 Nutri-Score update: tighter rules on sweeteners, red meat, and refined carbohydrates.
  • Silent recipe reformulation: how brands reworked ingredients to earn a higher grade.
  • Industry lobbying vs public health evidence: the political history of the 5-color badge.
Nutri-Score Deep Dive → Download Badges 📁
🔬 Processing & Additives

Ultra-Processed Foods (NOVA) & The Additive Maze

Beyond calorie counts: how industrial processing, texturizers, and emulsifiers disrupt gut health and metabolic function. Open Food Facts audits the ingredients list of millions of products.

Story Angles:

  • The NOVA 4 trap in organic and plant-based aisles: meat substitutes packed with ultra-processed additives.
  • Nitrites in deli meats, titanium dioxide (E171), and sweeteners: how citizen data alerted consumers before regulatory bans.
  • The additive cocktail effect: assessing multiple combined emulsifiers in children's diets.
Guide to NOVA → NOVA Assets 📁
🌿 Planet & Climate

The Green-Score: Measuring The Planetary Impact of Food

Food generates 25-30% of global greenhouse emissions. Open Food Facts combines ADEME Agribalyse Life-Cycle Analysis (LCA) with packaging recyclability and deforestation indicators.

Story Angles:

  • Single-use plastic & overpackaging: calculating the true packaging waste generated by weekly groceries.
  • Imported deforestation: tracing palm oil and soy risk across global supermarket brands.
  • Organic vs conventional farming: quantifying real biodiversity and carbon footprint differences.
Green-Score Methodology → Green-Score Badges 📁
🏷️ Purchasing Power

Open Prices: Crowdsourcing Inflation & Shrinkflation

Facing the grocery affordability crisis, citizens upload receipts to build an open, real-time observatory of price disparities across supermarket chains and territories.

Story Angles:

  • Shrinkflation exposed: smaller packages sold at higher unit prices, tracked across time.
  • Store price gouging: identical items differing by up to 40% between store locations of the same chain.
  • Everyday family basket inflation vs official macro indexes.
Open Prices Portal → Open Prices Assets 📁
📦 Circular Economy

Open Products Facts: Defragmenting Product Data to Fluidify the Circular Economy

Product data fragmentation across proprietary silos and incompatible formats is one of the greatest bottlenecks to repair, reuse, and recycling. Open Products Facts provides the universal open data commons needed to break data silos and fluidify the circular economy.

Story Angles:

  • Open Digital Product Passports (DPP): ensuring the circular transition is powered by open public commons rather than locked into proprietary software monopolies.
  • Repairability & spare parts linkage: connecting every consumer product's universal barcode to teardown guides, repairability scores, and spare part catalogs.
  • Material traceability & recycling streams: documenting exact polymer compositions, recyclable fractions, and hazardous components to supercharge automated sorting and industrial remanufacturing.
Open Products Facts → Open Products Facts Assets 📁

Global Infrastructure for Epidemiological Science

Over 1,000 peer-reviewed papers (Inserm, INRAE, Harvard, Oxford) rely on Open Food Facts data to advance public health, nutrition science, and evidence-based policy.

Story Angles:

  • Major cohort studies: correlation between consumption of ultra-processed foods and chronic disease risks.
  • Citizen science filling the void left by closed corporate datasets.
  • How independent European scientific committees evaluated and refined front-of-pack algorithms.

The Wikipedia of Food: Ethical AI & Open Source Commons

Proving an open, non-profit community can out-innovate corporate silos using open-source Flutter apps, computer vision, in-house OCR, and collective intelligence.

Fact Sheet & Boilerplate

Ready-to-Cite Cheat Sheet for Writers

Verified definitions, succinct background paragraphs, and foundational facts ready to copy directly into your drafts.

"Open Food Facts is an independent, non-profit open database of food products from around the world, empowering citizens to make healthier and more sustainable food choices."
Founded in 2012 by Stéphane Gigandet and co-founded with Pierre Slamich, presided over by Anca Luca, Open Food Facts is an independent, non-profit public interest organization that maintains the world's largest open, crowdsourced database of food products. Entirely free of food industry ownership and funded by individual donations and philanthropic grants, Open Food Facts catalogs over 3.6 million products across 180 countries under the Open Database License (ODbL). A pioneer of front-of-pack nutritional labeling (Nutri-Score), environmental metrics (Green-Score), Open Products Facts, and supermarket price crowdsourcing (Open Prices), the platform powers over 200 external mobile applications and provides critical research data cited in over 1,000 international peer-reviewed scientific studies (including Inserm, INRAE, and Harvard).
