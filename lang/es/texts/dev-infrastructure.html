Infrastructure, Systems & Bot Defense

Open Food Facts serves hundreds of millions of monthly requests as a global open data commons. Our volunteer and staff infrastructure team manages high availability, multi-datacenter clusters, and the critical front-line battle against rogue scrapers and aggressive AI crawler bots.

🤖 Taming Scrapers & Aggressive AI Crawlers

Open Food Facts is 100% open: our entire database is freely downloadable via daily bulk dumps at /data. Yet commercial scrapers and AI crawler bots frequently hammer live web pages, heavy search facets, and image servers with hundreds of thousands of unthrottled requests.

  • Burst Traffic Mitigation: Preventing uncoordinated scrapers from evading caches and exhausting database workers.
  • Guarding Public Access: Ensuring that mobile app users in grocery aisles and active contributors experience sub-second response times without 504 gateway errors.
  • Educating Reusers: Directing data consumers to our free bulk exports (JSON, CSV, Parquet) rather than harvesting HTML pages.

Nginx Reverse Proxy & Throttling

Nginx Rate Limiting Microcaching

All external traffic traverses specialized Nginx reverse proxies designed to withstand massive bursts and isolate critical endpoints.

  • Dynamic rate limiting per IP and per endpoint to throttle unbuffered burst requests
  • Image rate limiting and caching across dedicated static nodes
  • Country-level traffic shaping and microcaching for hot catalog queries
  • Nginx reverse proxy configurations managed collaboratively in Git

Fail2ban & Firewall Jails

fail2ban nftables iptables

Automated and persistent bot mitigation to drop malicious scanners and non-compliant scrapers at the kernel network layer.

  • Automated nginx-botsearch filters to trap blind vulnerability and exploit probes
  • Custom nginx-manual-ban persistent jails using SQLite storage surviving reboots
  • Real-time traffic aggregation scripts (concatenate_by_ip.pl) for rapid incident response
  • CIDR range banning for rogue cloud bot subnets
Crawler Mitigation Guide

Proxmox VE & ZFS Cluster

Proxmox VE ZFS LXC & KVM

Open Food Facts runs on bare-metal physical servers orchestrated with Proxmox VE, hosting virtual machines and lightweight LXC containers.

  • Automated ZFS replication across datacenters (OVH, Free, Moji) for disaster resilience
  • Docker and Docker Compose environments for containerized microservices
  • Server configuration managed declaratively through Git repositories

Observability & Upptime

Prometheus Grafana Upptime

Full-stack monitoring gives our ops engineers real-time visibility into latency, throughput, error rates, and system anomalies.

  • Transparent public status page powered by Upptime at status.openfoodfacts.org

  • Grafana dashboards for real-time traffic monitoring and incident post-mortems
