A simple, science-backed way to compare food quality in grocery stores at a single glance, without barriers.

2014 Historic First

The Pioneer 5-Color System

When the "5-color nutrition system" was first proposed in a 2014 French scientific report, Open Food Facts asked Prof. Serge Hercberg for the formula. We immediately computed the grades across all 10,000 French products in our database overnight, demonstrating that automated, objective front-of-pack scoring was achievable at scale without waiting years.

Read the 2014 Launch Blog Post (FR) ➔
Research Partnership

Colaborando con el equipo de investigación EREN

Open Food Facts worked closely with the public epidemiological research team EREN (Inserm / INRAE / CNAM / Université Sorbonne Paris Nord) led by Prof. Serge Hercberg and Dr. Chantal Julia. We shared field data, validated score calculations against scientific criteria, and supported academic studies on dietary quality and public health.

Mobile Pioneer

Mostrando el Nutri-Score en móvil

The Open Food Facts app for iPhone and Android was the very first mobile app to compute and display the Nutri-Score on barcode scans. Since 2014, it has empowered over 15 million citizens to decode supermarket shelves and compare nutritional quality in real time.
