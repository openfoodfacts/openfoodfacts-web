Our Cardinal Development Objectives

Every line of code written at Open Food Facts serves a clear mission: empowering citizens with transparent food data for health and the planet.

♿

Accessibility for Everyone

Making informed food decisions is a universal right. We build software that is fully usable by people who rely on screen readers, high-contrast modes, and assistive hardware across all devices.

👉

Worldwide Availability

Food culture and food labeling vary globally. We build support for hundreds of languages, all writing systems (RTL, Cyrillic, Hanzi, Arabic), diverse packaging norms, and regional nutritional systems.

🚀

Planetary Scale Impact

New features are shipped directly to millions of citizens, scientific researchers, and public policy makers. Your contributions create tangible, real-world change from day one.

Mentoring & Learning Opportunities

Learn, mentor, and build open source and open data together in a supportive community.

☀️ Google Summer of Code

Open Food Facts is a proud, recurring mentoring organization for Google Summer of Code (GSoC). Students and beginner developers collaborate closely with senior maintainers on high-impact projects across Flutter, AI/ML, and web development.
