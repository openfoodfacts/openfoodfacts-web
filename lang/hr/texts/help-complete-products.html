Pomozite nam dovršiti proizvode i poboljšati prehranu i zdravlje svih!

Information you add will be used by millions of people to make better food choices and by public health research teams for their studies.

Kako bismo utvrdili nutritivnu kvalitetu proizvoda pomoću Nutri-Scorea, analizirali njegov stupanj prerađivanja pomoću NOVA-e ili utvrdili je li pogodan za vegane ili vegetarijance, potrebno je:

  • kategorizirati proizvod
  • izvući popis sastojaka (često se to može vrlo lako učiniti odabirom i izrezivanjem popisa sastojaka na fotografijama proizvoda i automatskim izvođenjem teksta s fotografije)
  • Dodajte vrijednosti iz tablice nutritivnih vrijednosti.
Analiza sastojaka:

Filling in other information like the product name, the brand, the labels (e.g. organic and fair trade), the origins of ingredients, the type of packaging etc. also enables even more features and comparisons.

We need you!

We need your help to make the most of the product photos and data that we already have on Open Food Facts.

Here is how you can help us:

  1. Create an account on the Open Food Facts web site (if you have installed our mobile app, you may already have created an account which also works on the web site) and log-in.
  2. Select a product on the list of products to be completed.
  3. Kliknite na Uredi stranicu na vrhu stranice proizvoda.
  4. I pomozite popuniti neke od informacija:
    • Fill in the basic details like product name, brand and quantity
    • Odaberite kategoriju za proizvod
    • Odaberite i izrežite popis sastojaka i tablicu nutritivnih vrijednosti s fotografija koje imamo za proizvod.
    • Use the automatic text recognition for the ingredients and correct the spelling if the computer had trouble do extract all of them.
    • Ispunite nutritivne podatke kako bismo mogli izračunati Nutri-Score.
  5. Join us on our Slack to chat and exchange with other contributors, ask questions or take part in our special operations!
