Open Food Facts is a collaborative project with contributors from all around the world.

The project was started in 2012 by Stéphane Gigandet, creator of the food blogs portals Recettes de Cuisine (French) and various other collaborative and/or food-related projects, quickly joined by many others.

Od 2014. godine, budući da je Open Food Facts već brzo rastao, osnovana je nevladina organizacija koja podržava projekt.

Doprinosioci

Doprinositelji projektu Open Food Facts su ljudi koji su strastveni prema hrani, otvorenim podacima, zdravlju, okolišu ili nekoj od mnogih tema kojima se projekt bavi. They add products to the database and they are also the primary source for improvements and ideas.

Stalni tim

In 2019, Stéphane and Pierre started working on Open Food Facts full time, as the challenges facing Open Food Facts grew exponentially that year, with for instance 1 million additional users added in just one night. Naš prvi sponzor bila je Santé publique France, francuska agencija za javno zdravstvo, koja je prepoznala naš rad na Nutri-Scoreu. Since then, the permanent team has grown to around 7 members, to support the community on topics like infrastructure, core software tasks, product management, fundraising, partnerships and community. Pročitajte wiki stranicu o stalnom timu.

Teams (by focus)

The volunteers have created their own teams, on various topics ranging from taxonomies to prices. Read the wiki page on the various teams

Open Food Facts is a project open to all: you can join us by creating an account in less than a minute, you can download the mobile app, and with it, you can add your first product in a few more minutes, by taking photos with your mobile phone.

Pridružite nam se i na raznim društvenim mrežama kako biste saznali više o projektu i/ili započeli razgovor!
