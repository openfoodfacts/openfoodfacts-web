Serving millions of real-time queries daily incurs real costs in servers, database clusters, bandwidth, and maintenance. If your application, startup, research project, or company saves time and builds value with our API, please consider supporting Open Food Facts financially. Your contribution keeps our open infrastructure fast, resilient, and freely accessible to everyone worldwide.
