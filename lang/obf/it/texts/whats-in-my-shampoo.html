Why Decipher the Bathroom Cabinet?

Unlike food where ingredients are expressed in familiar words (wheat, sugar, olive oil), personal care items mandate INCI nomenclature: an obscure blend of Latin botanical names and chemical codes. Without a degree in chemistry, it is virtually impossible for shoppers to know what touches their scalp and hair every morning.

Open Beauty Facts was built to bridge this knowledge gap: translating 'Aqua' into water, highlighting phenoxyethanol, parabens, stripping sulfates (SLS/SLES), and the 26 mandatory fragrance allergens regulated under European consumer law.

Core Insights from the Shampoo Expedition

🧴 Why is the ingredient list on shampoo bottles so difficult to understand?
💡 Unlike food, personal care items mandate INCI nomenclature (Latin botanical names and complex chemicals). The mission translates obscure codes into plain language.
🧴 How many ingredients does an average bottle of shampoo contain?
💡 Minimalist organic formulas count fewer than 10 ingredients, while mainstream retail shampoos typically average 20 to 35, with some exceeding 40 distinct chemicals!
🧴 What preservatives and allergens are hidden in hair care products?
💡 Spotting parabens, phenoxyethanol, stripping sulfates (SLS/SLES), and the mandatory 26 EU fragrance contact allergens.
🧴 Why start with shampoos to launch Open Beauty Facts?
💡 Every household shower has shampoo: it served as the ideal Trojan Horse before expanding to soaps, toothpastes, moisturizers, and cosmetics.
