Doprinesite Open Beauty Facts

Sve što vam treba je scan-do stav! :-)

Can we make the cosmetics industry more open and transparent? Yes we scan! In barely a week, already 30 Open Beauty Facts contributors and growing !

Everyone can contribute

Be part of our collaborative, free and open database of cosmetic products from around the world!

Open Beauty Facts is a non-profit project made entirely by volunteers, we do need you.

Dodaj proizvode

Use the Open Food Facts app for Android or iPhone to easily scan the barcode of cosmetic products from your home or local stores and upload pictures of their label.

Mobilne aplikacije su spojene, pa sada možete skenirati kozmetiku, hranu za kućne ljubimce i druge proizvode u istoj aplikaciji Open Food Facts!

Nemate pametni telefon? No problem: you can also use your camera to add products directly on the web site.

Instalirajte aplikaciju i doprinesite transparentnosti kozmetike.

Recite svijetu

Sviđaju li vam se Činjenice o otvorenoj ljepoti? Recite drugima o tome!

Možete predstaviti projekt svojoj obitelji i prijateljima, pokazati im kako instalirati aplikaciju i doprinijeti, napisati objavu na blogu, podijeliti Činjenice o otvorenoj ljepoti na društvenim mrežama...

I izvedite to uživo! We have presented Open Beauty Facts to very different audiences in very different settings, and we always get a very enthusiastic response when we show what can be done when the data for products is open. We have lots of presentation materials (slides etc.) that you can adapt or present as-is.

Make it local

You can help to translate the site and mobile app in your language, and to translate presentations, announcements etc.

Možemo surađivati na podršci i dodavanju logotipa lokalnih oznaka, dešifriranju kodova lokalnih paketera kako bismo mogli mapirati proizvode itd.

Start or join a local contributors community: add local products, recruit friends, present the project in local meetups and conferences etc.

Potrebna je stručna pomoć

Razvoj Open Beauty Facts također zahtijeva specijalizirano znanje i stručnost u mnogim različitim područjima:

  • Project management

    Imamo hrpu ideja, a vi ih vjerojatno imate još više, no priličan je izazov odrediti prioritete, izraditi plan rada i voditi projekte kada je svaki sudionik volonter s često ograničenim raspoloživim vremenom.

  • Design

    Potrebna nam je pomoć u izgradnji boljeg korisničkog iskustva na web-stranici i mobilnoj aplikaciji Open Beauty Facts, u poboljšanju njihovog dizajna, u izradi upečatljivih prezentacijskih materijala itd.

  • Razvoj

    We have a lot of development work to do: on the Open Beauty Facts backend (Perl and MongoDB), API (JSON), web site (HTML5, JS, Foundation), iOS and Android apps (currently Apache Cordova / Phonegap, with a native Android version almost ready and much work to do on iOS), to build new cool reuses etc. Our code is on GitHub (Android, iOS, Cordova.)

  • Izgradnja zajednice

    Moramo izgraditi lokalne zajednice u svim zemljama i istovremeno ih globalno ujediniti.

    Vrlo je teško pokrenuti lokalnu zajednicu u nekoj zemlji bez da tamo živite, stoga je vaša pomoć u pronalaženju prvih vrlo motiviranih sudionika ključna. Jesi li i ti jedan od njih?

  • Komunikacija

    We are not sure what to write here, could you help?

    More seriously, there are lot of cool things that users, contributors and reusers do with Open Beauty Facts, it would be great to get more people to know about it. In particular, we need help for public and media relations.

  • Posebni projekti

    There are lots of interesting and original applications of cosmetic open data that we could work on with government cosmetic agencies, cosmetic producers, researchers, universities, schools, NGOs etc. Možda već imate ideje? Ako to učinite, molimo vas da nam pomognete da ih gurnemo naprijed.

Hajde da razgovaramo!

U našoj zajednici suradnika dijele se brojne ideje, energija i entuzijazam, pridružite nam se!

Evo nekoliko mjesta gdje možete upoznati druge suradnike, razgovarati s njima i raditi s njima:

  • Slack

    Slack je najbolji način za interakciju i suradnju s drugim suradnicima, programerima i ponovnim korisnicima. To je forum za raspravu kojem možete pristupiti iz svog preglednika ili telefona. Potpuno je promijenilo način na koji radimo zajedno. Please try it, click on the button below to get invited to our Slack.

  • Facebook groups

    We have Facebook groups in many languages for Open Beauty Facts contributors. Joining them is a good way to get news about the project and to share announcements to a wider audience.

  • Wiki

    We also have a wiki that we use to collaboratively document Open Beauty Facts and its sub-projects. We also use the wiki to manage translations and to work on the taxonomies / hierarchies of categories, labels etc.

There are many more ways to contribute to Open Beauty Facts, please join us on Slack (in the #openbeautyfacts channel) and let's start the discussion!
