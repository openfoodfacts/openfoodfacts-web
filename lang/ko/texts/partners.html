Ethics & Independence

Our independence: A cornerstone of trust

Why do citizens, scientists, and regulators trust Open Food Facts? Here is our clear framework for independence and funding integrity.

We are strictly independent from the food industry.

Open Food Facts does not accept any financial support, sponsorship, or governance participation from food corporations, agribusinesses, or retail conglomerates. This principle is strictly non-negotiable.

This complete firewall ensures total objectivity: our algorithmic calculations for Nutri-Score, Green-Score, and NOVA are 100% impartial and insulated from corporate lobbying.

Producer Collaboration: 100% Free Open Data →

Funding the commons vs. project-by-project funding

Targeted public and philanthropic grants (Google.org, European Commission / NLnet, Santé publique France, ADEME, France 2030, Afnic Foundation) fund specific technological breakthroughs (mobile app, Green-Score, search engine, crowdsourced prices, data defragmentation, horizontal scaling).

However, the vital spine of the project (servers, infrastructure, backups, permanent team) relies on unrestricted individual citizen micro-donations. General donations keep our foundation resilient.

🗓️ 2019 – Present

The European Commission (through the Next Generation Internet - NGI initiative) and the renowned NLnet Foundation fund Open Food Facts across 6 strategic milestones to build a privacy-first, sovereign, and human-centric digital commons:

12/2019 NGI0 Discovery

Personal Food Facts

Privacy-protecting personalized search & dietary alerts computed locally on-device without leaking sensitive health or allergen data to third parties.

nlnet.nl/project/OpenFoodFacts ↗
10/2020 – 05/2022 NGI0 Discovery

Folksonomy Engine for Food

Collaborative tagging engine allowing citizens, researchers, and innovators to add flexible, community-defined properties to food items beyond static taxonomies.

nlnet.nl/project/FolksonomyEngine ↗
06/2024 NGI0 Commons Fund

Pomme d’API

Major modernization of the Open Food Facts API: OpenAPI specifications, structured schemas, interactive documentation, and streamlined tools for 250+ ecosystem apps.

nlnet.nl/project/Pomme-dAPI ↗
06/2024 NGI0 Commons Fund

Open Everything Facts

Extending the open commons beyond food to empower informed consumer choices on all barcoded consumer goods (cosmetics, pet food, home products).

nlnet.nl/project/OpenEverythingFacts ↗

Open Prices - Scaling Price Collection

World's first crowdsourced open price database: machine learning tools to extract prices and barcodes from store shelf photos, validation pipelines, and community moderation.

Active Partner • 3 Major Programmes

ADEME

🗓️ 2020 – Present

The French Agency for Ecological Transition collaborates actively with Open Food Facts across multiple core initiatives to advance environmental transparency:

Agribalyse & Environmental Cost: Integrating Agribalyse Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) data to compute the Green-Score and model the true ecological cost of food in support of official environmental labeling.
Plein Pot sur les Emballages (2023): Participatory citizen-science campaign funded by ADEME documenting packaging materials, weights, and recyclability on 10,000+ products to fight overpackaging.
Appel à Communs (2025-2026): Laureate of ADEME's Open Commons Call to extend the Green-Score calculation to cooking recipes and collective catering.
https://nlnet.nl/

🌳 ENVOL VERT
Active Partner • Biodiversity

Envol Vert (Empreinte Forêt)

🗓️ 2021 – Present

Strategic partnership with the NGO Envol Vert to incorporate its 'Forest Footprint' (Empreinte Forêt) algorithm directly into Open Food Facts. This tool calculates and reveals imported deforestation risks across key commodities (livestock soy, palm oil, cocoa, coffee) on over 128,000 food products, empowering consumers and paving the way for EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) transparency.

Empreinte Forêt : Imported Deforestation Risk Algorithm
