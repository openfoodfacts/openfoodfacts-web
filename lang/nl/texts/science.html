Core Foundations

Our Three Scientific Pillars

Een enorme open, crowdsourced database van voedselproducten, de Wikipedia van voedsel

🥗

1. Science-Based Scoring

We do not invent proprietary scores. We strictly implement official, peer-reviewed public health metrics: Nutri-Score (EREN / Inserm), NOVA food processing classification (Prof. Carlos Monteiro, USP), and Eco-Score / Green-Score (ADEME Agribalyse), making complex science understandable in grocery store aisles.

🤖

2. Technology at Scientific Scale

Our open-source platform combines citizen crowdsourcing with optical character recognition (OCR), neural vision networks, and natural language processing to extract, structure, and validate nutritional declarations, ingredient lists, and additives on millions of products.

🌐

3. Returned to the Global Commons

All collected product data and computed indicators are redistributed under the Open Database License (ODbL). Researchers worldwide enjoy barrier-free, permanent access to high-fidelity food data without paywalls, commercial restrictions, or vendor lock-in.

Transparency & Commons

How Open Data Accelerates Scientific Research

The Wikipedia of food: turning messy real-world packaging into structured research infrastructure.

📊

Standardized Food Taxonomies

Open Food Facts organizes multi-country packaging data with standardized taxonomies for food categories, ingredients, allergens, food additives (E-numbers), nutrient reference values, and recycling instructions across 150+ countries.

🧬

Interdisciplinary Exploration

Beyond clinical nutrition, our database powers breakthroughs in computer science (optical character recognition, multimodal vision-language models), behavioral economics (supermarket nudge studies), and food sustainability (greenhouse gas footprints).

🔄

Reproducibility & Open Science

Scientific credibility demands reproducibility. Because Open Food Facts archives historic snapshots and open calculation source code, researchers can replicate findings, benchmark algorithms, and publish open datasets without commercial secrecy.
