Why It Matters

Food Education Belongs to Science, Not to Industrial Lobbies

For decades, corporate lobbies and trade interest groups (such as CEDUS for the sugar industry or CNIEL for industrial dairy) have created and distributed biased educational toolkits into public schools, presenting commercial interests under the guise of nutritional science.

At Open Food Facts, we believe students and young citizens deserve independent, scientifically validated food knowledge. Educational toolkits should not be authored by agro-industrial lobbies, but by public health bodies, independent researchers, and citizen commons.

Our mission is to equip schools with transparent tools (such as Nutri-Score, NOVA ultra-processing classifications, and Eco-Score) so that learners discover how to decipher marketing claims and make informed choices for their health and the environment.

Lobby-Sponsored School Kits
  • Promote specific industrial commodities and brands directly in classrooms
  • Downplay the health impacts of added sugars, additives, and ultra-processed food
  • Frame nutritional imbalances solely as an individual lack of exercise rather than industrial formulation
  • Obscure supply chain origins and environmental externalities
Open Food Facts Open Educational Commons
  • 100% non-profit & community-governed, funded strictly by public grants and individual donations
  • Based on official peer-reviewed public health algorithms (Santé Publique France, INRAE, Inserm, ADEME)
  • Teaches critical thinking and deciphering labels, barcodes, and marketing claims
  • Encourages open civic debate, hands-on scientific methodology, and transparency

Software Engineering & Bootcamps

Teaching Students to Build with Python

OpenClassrooms, a premier online training institution, integrates Open Food Facts into its Python developer certification track. Students build "PurBeurre", a full-stack Python application that queries our database to suggest healthier alternatives.

Inclusive Tech Training

Empowering Diverse Developers

Simplon.co provides economic opportunity through free, intensive tech training for job seekers and underrepresented communities. Students learn API consumption and database modeling with Open Food Facts to create useful public health tools.

Learn about Simplon.co
Data Science & Machine Learning

Training the Next Generation of Data Scientists

Open Food Facts is a Featured Dataset on Kaggle with thousands of public notebooks. Students and aspiring ML engineers use it to practice exploratory data analysis, natural language processing on ingredient lists, and predictive nutrition modeling.

Civic Engagement & Health

Auditing School & Campus Vending Machines

Through the #NutriScoreYourVendingMachine (#NoteTonDistrib) campaign, high school and college students photograph and grade snacks in campus vending machines, sparking constructive civic debate about healthier food choices.

Documented Impact

Empowering the Next Generation with Critical Food Literacy

When students analyze real-world nutrition and environmental data, learning transcends textbooks. They develop lifelong inquiry skills, understand the scientific method, and actively advocate for healthier public environments.

Scientific Foundation

Over 1,000 academic research studies rely on Open Food Facts data for epidemiological and environmental breakthroughs, providing students with rigorous, peer-reviewed scientific methodology.

Label & Media Literacy

Students learn to critically evaluate packaging claims, decipher complex E-number additives, and understand the difference between industrial marketing and nutritional reality.

Hands-on STEM & Coding

Real-world data connects programming, statistics, and biology with students' daily lives, making technical subjects practical, tangible, and socially meaningful.

Active Citizen Science

By photographing and contributing products, young people experience firsthand how collective open data can hold institutions and food manufacturers accountable.
