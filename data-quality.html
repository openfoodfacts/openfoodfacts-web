Philosophy & Architecture

Open, Incremental, and Auditable by Design

Unlike closed commercial catalogs that hide their error rates behind proprietary walls, Open Food Facts treats data quality as an open scientific discipline: every photo is inspectable, every calculation is reproducible, and every potential anomaly is publicly tagged so researchers and volunteers can filter or fix it immediately.

1 Understand (Measure & Historize)

Every night, all 4.2+ million products are evaluated against 218 automated data quality facets and tracked in our open analytics platform (Mirabelle / Apache Superset) across 6 core dimensions of quality.

📊 Daily Longitudinal Tracking

2 Act (Prevent, Highlight & Heal)

Verification never blocks contribution—instead, smart mobile & web nudges prevent typos upstream, Robotoff AI extracts ingredients and nutrition from photos, and anomalies are prioritized by scan popularity for rapid healing.

⚡ Popularity-Weighted Triage

3 Inform (Product & Dataset Transparency)

Every product page displays a transparent Data Quality Knowledge Panel showing photo recency, source verification, and any active warning or error tags—giving full confidence to shoppers, producers, and scientists.

🔍 1-Filter Research Exports
1. Completeness Are photos, ingredients, nutrition facts, and packaging fields present to compute Nutri-Score, Green-Score, and NOVA?
2. Reliability Are values internally coherent (thermodynamic energy balance, mass sum ≤ 100g, category & label consistency)?
3. Freshness How recently were product photos and formulations updated to reflect recipe changes and reformulations?
4. Structuration Are raw text strings mapped into our 30+ multilingual taxonomies (ingredients, additives, allergens, labels, categories)?
5. Exhaustivity What share of products on supermarket shelves are in the database? (98.9% of all barcode scans in France match!)
6. Representativity Does coverage reflect real-world consumption across countries, brands, and the Top 10,000 most-scanned items?
Defense-in-Depth Architecture

The 6 Firewalls Protecting Open Food Facts

Approximately 80% of contributions come from citizen contributors (web & mobile apps with timestamped photographic proof) and 20% from food producers and partner reuse apps (such as UFC-Que Choisir sending verified improvements back for 3+ years). Six independent firewalls ensure data integrity at every stage.

1 Positive Spirit of Contribution

We design against wrong incentives (no gamified rewards for inventing fake data) and guide contributors in real time with multilingual taxonomy autocomplete, unit validation, and instant warnings before a form is even saved.

🌱 Mistake-Proof UX Nudges

2 Everything Traced, Recorded & Versioned

Every single product modification is permanently versioned with contributor accounts or app identifiers. Sensitive edits require authentication, and patrollers can inspect full revision diffs and roll back vandalism in one click.

🕒 Wikipedia-Grade Audit Trail

3 Verifiability via Photographic Proof

Every product is anchored by timestamped photos of the front label, ingredient list, nutrition table, and packaging. Anyone can visually audit any number in the database against the physical package photograph in seconds.

📷 Ground-Truth Evidence

4 Human Patrol & Data Quality Daily

Our 50+ volunteer patrollers coordinate via monthly quality meetings, the #quality-data Slack channel, Nutri-Patrol moderation, and Data Quality Daily (DQD)—which sends 3 high-traffic flagged products per day to subscribers, weighted by scan count.

🕵️ ~6,000 Products Fixed / Month

5 Machine Patrol (218 Rules + Robotoff AI)

Every edit runs through DataQuality.pm and DataQualityFood.pm (218 deterministic checks) plus Robotoff AI (predicting across 3,800+ categories, spotting statistical outliers from category averages, and running AI Food Spellcheck).

316+ food producers (2,700+ brands, 55 automated pipelines) share 113,000+ products (25.3% of scans in France) via our free Pro Platform. Verified producer data is protected from anonymous overwrites while still undergoing our 218 quality checks.

Human-in-the-Loop Artificial Intelligence

How Robotoff AI, OCR & 218 Server Checks Work Together

At Open Food Facts, AI never replaces photographic ground truth—instead, Robotoff acts as a tireless co-pilot that extracts structured data from packaging photos, flags statistical outliers across 3,800+ food categories, and queues uncertain predictions in Hunger Games for rapid human verification.

Deterministic Physics + Statistical Category Models

While deterministic rules in DataQuality.pm enforce universal laws of physics and chemistry (such as Atwater thermodynamic energy reconstruction from fat, carbohydrates, fiber, protein, and polyols), Robotoff compares each product's nutrients against the statistical distribution of its exact category (e.g., comparing a salted butter or extra-virgin olive oil against thousands of peers) to surface subtle decimal shifts (`3.5g` vs `35g`).

🔤 State-of-the-Art OCR & AI Food Spellcheck

When a contributor snaps a photo of an ingredient list, OCR extracts the text and our AI Food Spellcheck automatically heals curved-packaging OCR typos, highlights any unrecognized sub-ingredients on the product edit page, and maps ingredients across 89.17% of products to compute NOVA, vegan/vegetarian status, and 260+ additives.

📋 Nutrition Table AI & Brand/Label Logo Detection

Our computer vision models crop nutrition tables from photos, extract per-100g nutrient values for human confirmation at hunger.openfoodfacts.org/nutrition, and automatically detect official certification logos (EU Organic, AB, Fairtrade, Nutri-Score, Triman, Green Dot).

🛡️ Real-Time Sensitive Image Guard & Nutri-Patrol

Uploaded images are screened in real time to block faces, explicit content, or non-food uploads, while Nutri-Patrol provides a distributed moderation interface so community patrollers can review flagged photos in seconds.

Reassurance & Transparency FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions from Researchers, Journalists & Contributors

Can anyone edit a product and introduce wrong data?

Open Food Facts works like Wikipedia combined with photographic proof and 218 automated physics/chemistry sensors: every contribution is anchored by timestamped photos of the physical packaging, every edit is versioned with one-click rollback, and any value that contradicts thermodynamic energy equations or category distributions is immediately flagged in data_quality_errors_tags. Furthermore, products shared directly by manufacturers on our Pro Platform have their verified attributes protected from anonymous overwrites.

How should scientific researchers filter Open Food Facts exports for epidemiological or nutritional studies?

Every nightly JSONL, CSV, and Parquet dump—as well as our Apache Superset analytics environment—includes the data_quality_errors_tags field. Researchers can exclude the ~3.2% of products with a flagged anomaly using a single filter (len(data_quality_errors_tags) == 0), ensuring that 100% of analyzed records pass all 218 automated consistency checks. Our team also collaborates directly with academic partners (EREN, INRAE, Santé publique France, Gustave Roussy) on category-specific audits.

How do you avoid false positives when products have legal nutrition rounding?

European and international labeling regulations allow manufacturers to round declared nutrition values, which can slightly shift the theoretical conversion between kilojoules and kilocalories ($1\text{ kcal} \approx 4.184\text{ kJ}$). Rather than flagging valid rounded labels, our DataQuality.pm engine uses a calibrated tolerance band ($4.0 \le \text{kJ}/\text{kcal} \le 4.4$) and accounts for fiber, alcohol, organic acids, and polyols before raising an error.

Where can I inspect the source code and join the Data Quality working group?

All quality rules are 100% open source in ProductOpener::DataQuality and ProductOpener::DataQualityFood, documented on the Data Quality Wiki Hub, and coordinated in the #quality-data channel on Open Food Facts Slack.
