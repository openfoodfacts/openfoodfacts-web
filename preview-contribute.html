Contribute to Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts is a global citizen movement and independent non-profit powering food transparency around the world. We are an international community of volunteers giving our time and talent to build an open common good for healthier living and a cleaner planet. 🌍🥗

To match your unique skills and interests with ongoing projects, we invite you to join our community or join our Contributors Skill Pool.

🌟 Quick Micro-Contributions

Everyone can contribute

Whether you have 2 minutes while grocery shopping or an afternoon to share, you can make a tangible difference in global food transparency.

📱 Mobile & Web

Add products

Use our free Android or iPhone app to easily scan barcodes from your pantry or grocery store shelves and upload clear photos of labels. No smartphone? You can snap photos with your camera and upload directly on the web.

Install Mobile App
🎮 Fun & Gamified

Complete products

Extract nutrition tables, crop ingredient lists, and classify food categories. Verify whether products are vegan, vegetarian, or gluten-free, or compete in our community games!

Play Hunger Games →
